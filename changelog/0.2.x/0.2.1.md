# 0.2.1 — 2026-10-18

## Added

- **`cern_opendata_get_analysis_env` `guides[].reference_definitions`** — the `[label]: url` lines from elsewhere in the guide that the quoted section links through, verbatim and in body order, rendered under their own fence; `section`, `section_truncated` and `body_offset` are unchanged ([#27](https://github.com/cyanheads/cern-opendata-mcp-server/issues/27)).
- **`cern_opendata_search_records` `subtypes_other_count` and `subcategories_other_count`** on `facets.type` and `facets.category` buckets whose secondary list the portal cut at 10, the portal's nested `sum_other_doc_count` relayed and shown as `· other values: N` ([#26](https://github.com/cyanheads/cern-opendata-mcp-server/issues/26)).

## Changed

- **A value past a cap is served at the cap** — `limit` on `cern_opendata_search_records` and `cern_opendata_search_trigger_paths` (50), `cern_opendata_list_files` (500) and `cern_opendata_get_validated_runs` (2,000) accepts up to 10,000, runs at the cap and says so in the notice; `cern_opendata_get_records` `ids` accepts up to 100, looks up the first 20 distinct and returns the rest under `deferred` after any budget-deferred inputs ([#23](https://github.com/cyanheads/cern-opendata-mcp-server/issues/23)).
- **`cern_opendata_get_records` and `cern-opendata://record/{recid}` cache the records they resolve** for 15 minutes (up to 8 MiB), so a `deferred` or `body_offset` follow-up makes no portal request ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/cern-opendata-mcp-server/issues/18)).
- **Framework sync** — skills, scripts, CodeQL per-language matrix, ignore files, a musl-prune step in the `Dockerfile` and `runtimeHint: npx` in `server.json`.
- **Dependencies** — `@cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` ^0.13.10 → ^0.13.15, `pino-pretty` ^13.1.3 → ^13.2.0, `zod` ^4.6.5 → ^4.6.7, `@biomejs/biome` 2.5.14 → 2.5.15, `@types/node` 26.6.3 → 26.6.5, `@vitest/coverage-istanbul` 5.0.2 → 5.0.4, `vitest` ^5.0.2 → ^5.0.4, `ignore` ^7.0.10 → ^7.0.12, `tsc-alias` ^1.9.5 → ^1.9.7, Bun 1.4.2 → 1.4.3.

## Fixed

- **A JSON-stringified list** (`"[\"CMS\",\"ATLAS\"]"`) is read as that array by every list filter of `cern_opendata_search_records` and by `cern_opendata_get_records` `ids`, instead of being split on its commas ([#29](https://github.com/cyanheads/cern-opendata-mcp-server/issues/29)).
- **HTML tables in `content[]`** render one row per line with cells joined by ` | `, and an anchor the HTML parser closes at its cell keeps its URL, so record 3700's menu links appear ([#22](https://github.com/cyanheads/cern-opendata-mcp-server/issues/22)).
- **Input descriptions** offer only the spellings the advertised schema accepts: the three `recid` fields, the list fields and `path` on `cern_opendata_search_trigger_paths`; the server still normalizes the rest ([#20](https://github.com/cyanheads/cern-opendata-mcp-server/issues/20)).
- **`cern_opendata_get_records` description** states that the first record always comes back whole past the 64,000-byte budget ([#24](https://github.com/cyanheads/cern-opendata-mcp-server/issues/24)).
- **`cern_opendata_search_trigger_paths` description** no longer says prescale tables are unpublished; it names the HLT menu record's `PrescaleService` block and the CMS luminosity records' prescale files ([#25](https://github.com/cyanheads/cern-opendata-mcp-server/issues/25)).
